# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Initial release.
- In-game menu on F8, styled after the game's stat panel.
- Live stat editing with sliders and `-` / `+` buttons.
- "Your Stats" tab matching the game's stat panel, plus an "Additional Modifiers" tab.
- XP multiplier and end-of-run gold multiplier (x1 to x10 in steps of 0.5).
- Optional "Keep stat bonuses between runs".
- F9 cycles the menu font.
